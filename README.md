## Indian E-Commerce Pricing Strategy and Revenue Growth | Data Science Project

## Data Science

### Project Overview

Developed an end-to-end **Data Science and E-Commerce Analytics project** analyzing 36 months of Indian e-commerce sales data to investigate pricing strategies, discount effectiveness, revenue growth, customer purchasing behavior, and market performance.

Applied Python-based data preprocessing, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), statistical analysis, customer segmentation, and data visualization to identify commercial trends and generate actionable insights for pricing optimization, revenue planning, and strategic business decision-making.

## Project Highlights

1. **End-to-End Data Cleaning & Preprocessing:** Processed 36 months of e-commerce transaction data by handling missing values, removing duplicate records, validating data types, and ensuring data consistency for reliable statistical analysis.

2. **Revenue Growth & Sales Performance Analysis:** Investigated monthly and yearly revenue trends, sales distributions, and product-level performance to identify growth patterns, seasonal fluctuations, and potential revenue opportunities.

3. **Pricing Strategy & Discount Effectiveness:** Examined relationships between product prices, discount percentages, promotional campaigns, and sales performance to understand how pricing decisions influence customer demand and business revenue.

4. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Conducted comprehensive analysis across product categories, brands, customer demographics, and transaction patterns to uncover meaningful relationships and commercial trends.

5. **Customer Segmentation & Purchasing Behavior:** Analyzed customer groups by age, gender, and purchasing characteristics to understand differences in buying behavior and identify opportunities for more targeted marketing strategies.

6. **Regional Sales & Market Performance:** Evaluated revenue contributions and customer demand across Indian states and business zones to identify high-performing regions and potential market expansion opportunities.

7. **Competitive & Inventory Analysis:** Investigated competition intensity and inventory pressure in relation to pricing and sales performance to highlight factors relevant to product positioning and inventory planning.

8. **Statistical Analysis & Data Visualization:** Applied descriptive statistics and developed analytical visualizations using Matplotlib and Seaborn to communicate revenue patterns, discount distributions, regional comparisons, and customer trends.

9. **Business Insights & Strategic Recommendations:** Interpreted analytical findings to identify potential improvements in pricing strategies, promotional planning, customer targeting, and revenue growth initiatives.

## Technologies & Skills

- **Programming Language:** Python
- **Libraries:** Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn
- **Data Science:** Data Cleaning, Data Preprocessing, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), Descriptive Statistics, Data Visualization
- **Analytical Techniques:** Customer Segmentation, Trend Analysis, Pricing Analysis, Discount Effectiveness Analysis, Regional Analysis
- **Business Domain:** E-Commerce Analytics, Revenue Growth Analysis, Pricing Strategy, Customer Behavior Analysis, Competitive Analysis, Inventory Analytics

## Business Objective

To evaluate how pricing decisions, promotional discounts, customer demographics, regional demand, and competitive conditions relate to revenue performance within the Indian e-commerce market over a 36-month period.

The project aims to provide analytical insights that can support:

- Data-driven pricing and discount strategies.
- Identification of revenue growth opportunities.
- Improved customer segmentation and marketing decisions.
- Regional market performance evaluation.
- Better inventory and promotional planning.

## Business Value

This project demonstrates the practical application of **Data Science techniques to real-world e-commerce business challenges**, particularly pricing evaluation, revenue analysis, customer segmentation, and market performance assessment.

By transforming historical transaction data into structured analytical insights, the project provides a foundation for evidence-based commercial decision-making and future predictive analytics applications.
