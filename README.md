## Indian E-Commerce Pricing Strategy & Revenue Growth Analysis

## Data Science

### **Python | Data Cleaning | EDA | Statistical Analysis | Pricing Analytics | Revenue Optimization**

### Project Overview

Analyzed **36 months of Indian e-commerce sales data** using Python to investigate pricing strategies, discount effectiveness, revenue growth, customer purchasing behavior, and regional market performance.

Applied data cleaning, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), descriptive statistics, customer segmentation, and data visualization to transform transactional data into business-focused analytical insights.

## Business Problem

E-commerce companies face challenges in balancing competitive pricing, promotional discounts, customer demand, inventory availability, and revenue growth.

This project investigates how pricing patterns, customer demographics, regional markets, and promotional activities relate to sales performance and business growth.

## Project Objectives

- Evaluate pricing strategies and promotional discount patterns.
- Analyze monthly and yearly revenue trends.
- Examine product category and brand performance.
- Investigate customer purchasing behavior and demographic segments.
- Compare sales performance across Indian states and business zones.
- Explore competitive conditions and inventory pressure.
- Identify opportunities for data-driven revenue and pricing decisions.

## Project Highlights

1. **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing:** Prepared 36 months of transactional data by handling missing values, duplicate records, inconsistent formats, and data type issues.

2. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Investigated revenue distributions, product categories, customer demographics, sales trends, and purchasing patterns.

3. **Revenue Growth & Trend Analysis:** Examined historical sales performance to understand revenue movements, seasonal patterns, and business growth trends.

4. **Pricing & Discount Effectiveness:** Analyzed product prices, discount percentages, and promotional patterns to investigate their relationships with sales performance.

5. **Customer Segmentation:** Compared purchasing behavior across customer age groups, gender, and other available demographic attributes.

6. **Regional Market Analysis:** Evaluated revenue and purchasing patterns across Indian states and business zones to understand geographical differences in sales performance.

7. **Competitive & Inventory Analysis:** Explored competition intensity and inventory pressure as potential factors associated with pricing and revenue performance.

8. **Statistical Analysis & Visualization:** Used descriptive statistics, Matplotlib, and Seaborn to communicate revenue trends, pricing patterns, regional comparisons, and customer distributions.

9. **Business Insights & Recommendations:** Interpreted analytical findings to identify potential opportunities for pricing improvements, promotional planning, customer targeting, and revenue optimization.
